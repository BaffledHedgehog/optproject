Mathematical contract and changes from the preserved v15 scalar core

The model and sharpness scope are exactly those in proof_references.
The joint arguments are mathematical J(g,P), not the positional order
of the old private v15 entry point. All numerical controls below are
implementation checks, not new proofs or new optimization experiments.

For m>0, H0>=m, H1>0, h>0, put a=H0, b=H1*h and

  tau=(a+m+b/2)/(a+sqrt(a*a+b*(a+m)+b*b/2)),
  S(h)=a*(1/2-tau*tau)+(b/3)*(1-2*tau*tau*tau).

Theorem gapregime:main gives B(h)=h iff S(h)<=0. Corollary
gapregime:radius-monotone identifies the maximum over any restricted
radius interval at its left endpoint when S(h)<=0. The tangent bound
gapregime:tangent-upper gives V(h,rho)<=h+rho*S(h).

For P,g>0, h=min(g,P*P/m), the joint theorem therefore gives

  J(g,P)=V(h,h*h/(P*P)) <= h+(h*h/(P*P))*S(h)

when S(h)<=0. The general joint representation has radii
[h*h/(P*P),h/m]. If g>=P*P/m this interval is a singleton and the norm
branch already needs only one scalar node, independently of S(h).

The gate uses an outward interval [Slo,Shi]. Shi<=0 proves the regime,
Slo>0 proves the complementary regime, and every other interval refuses
the fixed-precision call. The optional algebraic bound uses exactly
h+(h*h/(P*P))*Shi, with final arithmetic performed in Fraction. There is
no claimed relative-envelope factor for this optional upper bound.

Relative-enclosure budgets

Let factor=theta>1, internal=(1+theta)/2, and K,L be outward upper bounds
from the preserved scalar kernel. The known lower bound for joint or
norm envelopes is h/2. The core node construction has additive switch
error at most 2*rho*K*2^(-n).

For the one-node joint regime choose

  n=ceil(log2(max(1,4*K/(m*(internal-1))))).

Since rho<=h/m, its switch allowance is at most (internal-1)*h/2.
There is no outer mesh allowance. The separately checked value-rounding
width is at most (theta-internal)*h/2. The existing finish_affine routine
then returns a rigorous lower/upper enclosure and checks U<=theta*Lout
with exact rational arithmetic.

Outside the regime, a=g*g/(P*P), b=g/m, width=b-a>0. The v16 restricted
grid uses J=max(1,ceil(2*L*width/((internal-1)*g))) intervals and
n=ceil(log2(max(1,8*K/(m*(internal-1))))). Consequently the mesh allowance
L*width/(2*J) and the inner allowance 2*(g/m)*K*2^(-n) are each at most
(internal-1)*g/4. This is the same finite enclosure proof as the retained
v15 grid, with its actual restricted width instead of the larger g/m.

For B(g) in the nonpositive regime the result is exact, with no root or
grid. Outside it the unchanged v15 gap grid is used. H1=0 uses the exact
rational beta formula, beta=(H0*H0-2*m*H0-m*m)/(4*H0), already proved in
the constant-curvature joint theorem. All zero inputs precede division.

Cost and refusal contract

Every attempted regime scalar evaluation is counted before its square
root. Both the dependency guard (mpmath 1.3.0) and the scaled-input guard
precede this evaluation, including branches that never call bounds().
Exact formulas have their own count. Scalar-start totals include bounds,
switches, endpoint losses, regime evaluations and algebraic evaluations;
preflight budgets include work already spent on the new gate.

No finite-bit ability to decide every equality or arbitrarily close sign
is asserted. No unsuccessful partial trace is a completed certificate.
Keyword/signature errors remain ordinary Python errors. Handled exact
input, resource, interval-sign and rounding failures yield a structured
refusal retaining their diagnostic cost.
