MATHEMATICAL CHECKS
1. The constrained first-order inequality and full-gap certificate are
   correct for a convex function on a compact convex set, including boundary
   minimizers with nonzero gradients. No unknown minimum enters the algorithm.
2. The uniform curvature estimate follows from the integral gradient bound
   along segments and Gronwall; the L1=0 and zero-curvature cases are handled.
3. The product-family invariance is exact with the stated common initial
   curvature and tie rules. All directions lie in y=0, objective evaluations
   and retained lower bounds coincide, and the Hessian block is unchanged.
4. For f_A(x,y)=exp(x)+A*y and fixed L0=L1=1, every fixed-rule displacement
   before crossing zero is at most exp(a-1)/A. A valid stopping certificate
   cannot close for epsilon<1-exp(-1) while x>=0. This proves the stated
   ceil(a*exp(1-a)*A) lower bound. Capping the step cannot invalidate it.
5. Every backtracking search terminates once L>=K_Q. The accepted curvature
   recurrence, the count of updates above 2*K_Q, and the ordered phase
   argument are valid. The last step leaving a phase is included correctly.
6. The objective-query identity follows by telescoping accepted curvature
   ratios. One gradient and one full LMO are used at every visited point,
   including the final point, so their exact counts are N+1.
7. Under the explicitly stated directional-oracle bounds, carried precision
   gives the stated logarithmic refinement bound. Every accepted nonterminal
   slope is at least kappa times the retained error bound. The phase proof
   and the exact directional-query count N+1+R therefore follow.
